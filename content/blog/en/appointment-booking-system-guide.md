# Appointment Booking System: How to Choose the Right One for a Service Business

Hair salons, estheticians, therapists, and consultants all share the same problem: their calendar is split between a notebook, WhatsApp, and memory. One forgotten appointment, two customers booked for the same slot, or a reminder that never went out — all of it costs money and time. A good booking system doesn't need to be complicated, but it does need to cover a few basics.

## The problem with managing appointments by phone and chat

When appointments are managed through calls and messages, there's no single source of truth. The customer isn't always sure whether their appointment is confirmed, and the business owner has to remember to check every conversation separately. Busy moments — a holiday eve, a weekend — are exactly where mistakes happen.

## What a good appointment system needs

- **A clear availability calendar** — the customer only sees real open slots and can't book a time that's already taken.
- **Appointment confirmation** — the ability for the business owner to approve a booking before it's locked into the calendar, to avoid surprises.
- **A clear message to the customer** — so they know exactly when and where their appointment is.
- **One place for every appointment** — no digging through old chats.

- Can you require manual appointment confirmation when you need to?
- Does the customer get a clear confirmation of their booking?
